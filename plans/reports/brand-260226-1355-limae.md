# Brand Guideline: Limae

**Version:** 0.1 | **Date:** 2026-02-26

---

## 1. Brand Essence

**Tagline:** *The final file. Before you publish.*

**Etymology:** Latin *lima* (the file) — Horace's *limae labor* ("the labor of the file") meant careful literary revision. That's exactly what this product does.

**One-liner:** Limae is the final step before you publish — AI-powered grammar fix, navigation repair, and image generation for your ebook.

**Brand archetype:** The Craftsman — precise, scholarly, quietly confident. Doesn't shout. Delivers results.

**Brand promise:** Every ebook that goes through Limae comes out cleaner, sharper, and ready to publish.

---

## 2. Tone of Voice

| Attribute | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Precise** | Say exactly what you mean; no vague promises | "Fixed 47 grammar errors" not "improved your writing" |
| **Warm** | Authors are creatives — speak to the human, not the file | "Your book is ready" not "Processing complete" |
| **Confident** | No hedging; back up claims with specifics | "Passes EPUBCheck validation" not "should work on most readers" |
| **Brief** | Every word earns its place — mirror the editing product | Short sentences. No filler. |

**Voice DO:** "Upload your ebook. We'll fix the rest."
**Voice DON'T:** "Leverage our cutting-edge AI-powered solution to enhance your manuscript experience."

## 4. Typography

### Wordmark / Display
- **Font:** Playfair Display (serif) — editorial authority, nods to print publishing
- **Weight:** Regular for wordmark, Bold for display headings
- **Usage:** Logo, hero headings, section titles

### UI / Interface
- **Font:** Inter — clean, readable, neutral
- **Weights used:** 400 (body), 500 (labels/captions), 600 (UI headings), 700 (CTAs)
- **Usage:** All interface elements, buttons, forms, tables

### Body / Editorial
- **Font:** Lora (serif) — warm, readable for longer copy
- **Usage:** Marketing copy, blog, feature descriptions

### Type Scale (rem base 16px)
| Level | Size | Weight | Font |
|-------|------|--------|------|
| Display | 48px | 700 | Playfair Display |
| H1 | 36px | 700 | Playfair Display |
| H2 | 24px | 600 | Inter |
| H3 | 18px | 600 | Inter |
| Body | 16px | 400 | Lora / Inter |
| Small | 14px | 400 | Inter |
| Label | 12px | 500 | Inter uppercase |

## 5. Spacing System

Base unit: **4px**

| Token | Value | Usage |
|-------|-------|-------|
| `space-1` | 4px | Tight internal spacing |
| `space-2` | 8px | Component internal padding |
| `space-3` | 12px | Small gaps |
| `space-4` | 16px | Standard gap |
| `space-6` | 24px | Section sub-spacing |
| `space-8` | 32px | Component separation |
| `space-12` | 48px | Section spacing |
| `space-16` | 64px | Hero / page-level breathing room |

**Border radius:** 6px for cards, 4px for inputs, 8px for buttons (rounded but not pill-shaped — precise, not playful)

## 6. Icon & Image Style

### Icons
- **Style:** Line icons, 1.5px stroke, rounded caps — Lucide or Phosphor icon set
- **Size:** 16px (inline), 20px (UI actions), 24px (feature icons)
- **Color:** Warm Gray (`#8A8680`) default; Limae Gold (`#C9A84C`) for active/accent

### Illustrations / Marketing Images
- **Style:** Minimal editorial illustrations — think book pages, ink strokes, subtle paper textures
- **NOT:** Stock photos of laptops, generic AI imagery, abstract blue blobs
- **Color:** Ivory + Midnight + Gold only — monochromatic with gold accent
- **Mood:** Craft, precision, the feel of a well-made book

### Product Screenshots
- **Frame:** Dark `#1A1F2E` browser frame or device mockup
- **Background:** Ivory (`#F8F5EF`) page background
- **Annotations:** Gold (`#C9A84C`) callout lines with Inter labels

---

## 7. Logo Direction

**Wordmark:** `Limae` in Playfair Display Regular — the serif carries the classical Latin weight naturally
**Mark concept (optional):** A stylized `L` with a fine horizontal stroke through the stem — evoking a proofreading correction mark and the filing motion
**Pronunciation note:** "LEE-may" — include on About page to prevent confusion
**Avoid:** Bookmarks, open books, quills — overused in publishing software
